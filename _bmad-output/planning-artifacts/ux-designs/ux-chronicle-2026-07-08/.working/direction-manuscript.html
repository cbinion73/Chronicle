Direction 1 · Manuscript — “The illuminated family Bible”

This is the boldest break: there is no app shell at all — no sidebar, no toolbar, no cards. A single ink thread runs down the left margin of every page and is the navigation, with the stones of a life set directly on it, the way a family Bible carries its record in the margins. One liturgical red is used exactly as medieval rubrication was: numerals, feast marks, and the grace note are written in red because they are the sacred interruptions of ordinary ink.
