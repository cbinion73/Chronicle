Direction 3 · Stone Court — “The stones are real objects”

The middle path: a quiet stone-gray sidebar keeps the familiar app shell, but every sacred object on the parchment is rendered as a physical stone — river-worn, weighty, each with its own irregular silhouette, top-light, and shadow. Prayers are small pebbles you could hold; life markers are standing stones set on a drawn line; and the answered prayer carries a warm inner glow, as if the light were inside the stone itself.
